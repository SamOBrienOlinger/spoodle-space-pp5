How SpoodleSpace uses information

SpoodleSpace is a community project by Sam O’Brien-Olinger, Sam Tim Solutions, Ireland. Privacy enquiries and requests about your personal information can be sent to samobrienolinger@gmail.com. Identify the account or record concerned without sending your password or unnecessary identity documents.

This is a social-sharing service, not a private medical or document vault. Published posts, profiles and dog records can be visible to others. The current dog-health API permits public reading and searching by vet name. Do not put your home address, personal medical details, identity documents, private phone numbers or confidential third-party information into a public record.

Information used and why

Accounts and authentication
Account identifiers, usernames, credentials and any profile/account details you supply are processed to create and secure the account, sign you in and let you manage your content. Passwords should never be shared in a post, message to the operator or browser draft. Account processing needed to provide requested account features relies on performance of the service relationship; proportionate security and abuse prevention rely on legitimate interests.
Posts, profiles and community interactions
Content you submit can include text, photographs, profile details, comments, likes, follows and dog-related records. The purpose is to publish and manage the community features you request. Public content can be read, copied or linked by other people; deleting it here cannot remove a copy independently saved elsewhere. Only upload material you are entitled to share.
Dog profiles, health and safety records
Dog records can be linked to your user account and can contain information about people as well as animals. The interface and API are not a guarantee of confidentiality. Use only information appropriate for the stated public/community purpose. Do not submit sensitive information about a person or information about a child that should not be public.
Unfinished post drafts
Optional browser session storage can remember a post title and text before submission. This is off until you allow it in Cookie settings. The draft is not an uploaded post. A selected photo is not stored by this draft-saving feature. Choosing Post or Save changes on the live service sends the content and any selected upload to the API.
Technical requests and correspondence
Hosting, API and media providers receive network information, such as IP addresses and requested resources, to deliver and secure the service. Email you send includes your address, message and attachments and is used to respond to the enquiry, based on the legitimate interest in communicating with people who contact the project. Sending an enquiry does not automatically subscribe you to marketing.

Live accounts versus demonstration previews

The GitHub Pages preview has demonstration features distinct from the live account service. A local composer preview is not publication to the live community. The privacy controls apply to optional draft storage in either environment. Follow the interface's live-account notices before entering account information.

Cookies and browser storage

The banner offers Reject optional, Choose settings and Accept optional with separate settings information. Rejecting optional storage does not disable sign-in or essential security features. No analytics or advertising service is enabled by this consent control.

spoodlespace:privacy:v1 — necessary local-storage record
Stores the notice version, time and optional draft permission when you choose. It does not contain a random advertising identifier. A choice is valid for 180 days, after which a subsequent check requests it again. Local storage does not physically remove itself while a browser is closed.
spoodle-post:<profile>:<record> and spoodle-preview-post — optional session storage
Store unfinished post text only after permission. The live key separates accounts and forms. Successful submission, Clear saved drafts, withdrawal or a subsequent check detecting expired/invalid permission removes the corresponding saved draft. Closing a tab normally ends session storage, although browser session restoration can retain it. Text still visible in an open form is not erased by withdrawing permission and is not saved again unless allowed.
my-app-auth and my-refresh-token — necessary API authentication cookies
The backend uses these cookies for the authenticated session and token refresh. They are separate from optional draft-saving. They are configured as secure, cross-site cookies to support the separate frontend and API origins. Expiry is determined by the backend-issued tokens and cookie attributes, not by the 180-day privacy-choice period. Sign out to end the application session; a frontend preference switch cannot delete an HTTP-only cookie held on the API domain.
Other necessary session/security state
Django session and CSRF features can use sessionid and csrftoken when those features are accessed. Authentication state needed to maintain a requested session is not an advertising preference. Cookie attributes and expiry are supplied by the server; contact the operator for the current deployment inventory.

To change or withdraw optional permission, return to the app and use the persistent Cookie settings button in its footer. Clear saved drafts removes stored draft copies in the current tab without changing your login. Browser controls can delete more site data but may also sign you out or affect other projects on a shared origin.

Providers and international processing

The repository configures Heroku hosting/API delivery, a database selected by the deployment configuration, and Cloudinary media storage/delivery. GitHub Pages is used for the separate static preview. Loading images or API responses sends connection data to the service providing them. Provider infrastructure and support can involve processing outside Ireland or the EEA; the applicable location, contract and transfer safeguards depend on the operator's current service configuration. Request current recipient and transfer details using the contact above rather than assuming all data stays in Ireland.

Relevant provider information: Heroku privacy information, Cloudinary privacy policy, and GitHub privacy statement. These provider notices do not replace the operator's responsibility for information it controls.

Retention, deletion and requests

Account and published record data persist in the service until changed or deleted, subject to any applicable security, backup or legal retention. Use available edit/delete controls for your records. For account deletion, an export, a record without a delete control or a question about retained copies, email the operator. No automatic deletion of Cloudinary assets, provider logs or backups is promised merely because a frontend record is deleted. The operator must check those systems when handling a request.

Retention of correspondence depends on resolving the enquiry, maintaining a necessary record or meeting an applicable legal requirement. Do not assume an automatic fixed deletion period for a message. Optional browser drafts follow the shorter lifecycle described above.

Your rights and complaints

You may request access to personal data, correction, erasure, restriction and portability where applicable, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. These rights are subject to legal conditions and exceptions. Consent to optional storage can be withdrawn at any time without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing. A privacy request does not require agreeing to optional cookies or sending your password.

The usual GDPR response period is one month, subject to lawful extensions and proportionate identity checks. You can complain to Ireland's Data Protection Commission. Contact the operator about inaccurate, harmful or inappropriately public content, including information concerning children.
